Phasemap

A free, open-source stereo phase and loudness meter by Nullzone Audio.

The next steps install the plugin for your DAW:

  • Audio Unit, VST3 and CLAP to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins
  • The standalone app to /Applications

Under Customize you can leave out formats you do not need. Installing again replaces the old version.

After installing, rescan plugins in your DAW. Help and source code: nullzone-audio.org
